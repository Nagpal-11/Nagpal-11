## 🤝 LEADERSHIP & IMPACT

<div align="center">

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/AI_CLUB_COORDINATOR-Mizoram_University-0d1117?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=0d1117&color=00fff9&logo=perplexity&logoColor=00fff9"/>

</div>

As **AI Club Coordinator at Mizoram University**, I lead a campus-wide movement to bring **practical, industry-relevant AI education** to students who have historically only had access to theory-heavy curriculums. My mandate:

- 🎯 **Design hands-on coding pipelines** — from raw data to deployed model — so students learn by *shipping*, not just reading.
- 🧭 **Architect learning tracks** around real project pipelines: data preprocessing → model design → training → deployment.
- 🔥 **Cultivate a builder culture** on campus, mentoring peers to think like engineers, not just exam-takers.
- 📚 **Bridge research and application**, translating cutting-edge deep learning concepts into workshops students can actually use.
